Questions

A few things to know.

Clear answers before you start. If you need something else, the care team is one message away.

Contact CarePoint
Do I need an account to book?

Yes. Create an account or log in before sending a request so you can follow it in My portal.

Can I change or cancel a visit?

Yes. Open My portal to manage a request that has not been completed. You can cancel it or check the current status.

What happens after I send a request?

The clinic reviews the requested time and updates the status. You can follow every change from your account.

Are all clinic details verified?

Clinic listings are managed by the clinics themselves and shown in the directory. Availability and specialties can vary.
